# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent to each other. This is a small project; a bit of courtesy goes a long way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume the other person is trying to help.
- Keep criticism about the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a feature, and that contributors may run out of time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to the GitHub repository, the Discord server, and any other space where you take part
as a member of this project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Blaketon/orchboard/security/advisories/new) or a
direct message on [Discord](https://discord.gg/JRHBcwzUag). Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions and block accounts that break these rules.
